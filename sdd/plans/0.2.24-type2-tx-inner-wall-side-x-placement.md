# Type2 TX Inner Wall-Side X Placement

## Intent
- Keep the active `tx_inner_rect_void_coil` attached to the wall side of its guide region: the smaller local/world X boundary of `tx_inner_region`.
- Preserve existing Y centering and top-Z placement for TX inner coil geometry, actual-region derivation, terminal metadata, and passive underlay/void-stack footprints.
- Remove `modeled_objects.tx_inner_rect_void_coil.x_position_ratio` as an effective sampled placement degree of freedom without changing unrelated RX or dormant TX outer behavior.

## Decisions
- `tx_inner_single_coil` placement is lower-X anchored: resolved design/actual min X equals `tx_inner_region.origin_xyz[0]`.
- `tx_inner_actual_region` continues to be derived from the same fit envelope as the modeled coil, so actual-region bounds and modeled physical bounds stay identical in X/Y.
- `x_position_ratio` remains a required compatibility table on TX inner single-coil TOML, but it must be fixed `[false, 0.0, 0.0, 1]`.
- Nonzero or sampled TX inner `x_position_ratio` fails at the modeled spec parser boundary.
- Sampling/build metadata must not report TX inner `x_position_ratio` as a sampled owner or design variable.
- RX `x_position_ratio` fixed-zero compatibility state and dormant TX outer ratio-placement helper behavior remain unchanged.

## Invariants
- TX inner X placement must not depend on seed, retry number, or sampled owner values.
- Changing `outer_x_usage_ratio` may change TX inner width, but the lower-X wall-side boundary remains fixed at owner min X.
- Existing fail-fast fit checks remain: the resolved design outer footprint must fit inside `tx_inner_region`.
- Tests must assert the placement contract through canonical ledger bounds, not by reverse-calculating downstream CAD geometry.

## Implementation Slots
- Slot A owns parser/spec/sampling surfaces in `src/peetsfea/type2_step_spec_modeled.py` and `src/peetsfea/type2_sampled_sampling.py`, plus sample/build tests that remove TX inner `x_position_ratio` from sampled owner/design-variable expectations.
- Slot B owns placement behavior in `src/peetsfea/type2_single_coil_scene.py` and export tests proving TX inner actual/model bounds stay lower-X anchored while Y remains centered.
- Slot C owns official examples and source-fixture updates: `examples/type2_sweep.toml`, TOML fixture text in sample/build tests, and example/test SDD notes.
- Slot D owns SDD/code-note alignment and focused validation cleanup for actual-region/export contracts, including stale wording that says TX inner uses sampled `x_position_ratio`.
