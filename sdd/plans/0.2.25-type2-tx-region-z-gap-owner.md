# 0.2.25 Type2 TX Region Z Gap Owner

## Goal
- Add `non_model_objects.tx_region.z_gap_from_rx_plane_mm` as the canonical non-modeled owner for TX guide Z placement.
- Keep the fixed example at `[false, 80.0, 80.0, 1]` so current fixed-example realized placement stays unchanged.
- Enable sweep sampling at `[false, 45.0, 130.0, 37]` in the active sweep example.

## Contract
- The reference plane is the common lower Z of `tv` and `rx_region_max`; mismatch is fail-fast.
- `tx_region.origin_z = reference_lower_z - z_gap_from_rx_plane_mm - tx_region.size_z`.
- `tx_region.origin_x`, `tx_region.origin_y`, and `tx_region.size_xyz` remain parser-owned source values.
