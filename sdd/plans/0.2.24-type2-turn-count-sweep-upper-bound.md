# 0.2.24 Type2 Turn Count Sweep Upper Bound

## Goal
- Lower active type2 TX/RX coil `turn_count` sweep upper bounds by one sampled step.
- Keep fixed singleton `turn_count` examples unchanged.
- Keep non-`turn_count` owners such as `equivalent_turn_count`, `tx_coil_count`, and `layer_count` unchanged.

## Boundaries
- Active official sweep examples change from `1..4 count=4` to `1..3 count=3` for:
  - `modeled_objects.tx_inner_rect_void_coil.turn_count`
  - `modeled_objects.rx_rect_void_coil.turn_count`
- Synthetic active type2 test fixtures that use `turn_count = 2..6 count=5` change to `2..5 count=4`.
- Xfailed generic plate-stack tests that declare `turn_count = 2..5 count=4` change to `2..4 count=3`.
- Legacy `type1` paths remain frozen and must not be edited.

## Work Distribution
- Worker A owns official examples plus example/setup SDD notes.
- Worker B owns sample-entry tests and the matching test SDD note.
- Worker C owns build-entry tests and the matching test SDD note.
- Worker D owns generate-step and generic plate-stack sampling tests plus matching SDD notes.

## Hazards
- Do not lower fixed singleton one-turn examples; that would make invalid or zero-turn coils.
- Do not rewrite broad numeric ranges that are not `turn_count`.
- Do not touch legacy `type1` files.
