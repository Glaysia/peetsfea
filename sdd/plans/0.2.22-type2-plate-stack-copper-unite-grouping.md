# 0.2.22 Type2 Plate-Stack Copper Unite Grouping

## Goal
- active `tx_plate_stack` / `rx_plate_stack` copper를 개별 stripe/bridge/stub final body로 남기지 않는다.
- export/import/setup-ready handoff에서 role당 정확히 하나의 united copper conductor body를 사용한다.
- ferrite-family와 동일한 방식으로 copper family도 role-group 기반으로 재생성 가능한 ledger contract를 가진다.

## Decisions
- 최종 exported/imported conductor body name은 role당 하나다.
  - TX: `tx_plate_copper`
  - RX: `rx_plate_copper`
- 기존 segment labels는 pre-unite source/provenance only다.
  - `*_copper_wall_t*`
  - `*_copper_coil_t*`
  - `*_bridge_s*`
  - `*_stub_in`
  - `*_stub_out`
- final plate-stack body order는 role-local 6-body contract다.
  - `<tx|rx>_plate_copper`
  - `<tx|rx>_pcb_wall`
  - `<tx|rx>_stack_pet_psa`
  - `<tx|rx>_stack_ferrite`
  - `<tx|rx>_stack_air`
  - `<tx|rx>_pcb_coil`
- `expected_exported_body_groups`는 role당 copper group과 ferrite group을 모두 가진다.
  - TX: `g_copper_tx -> [tx_plate_copper]`, `g_ferrite_tx -> [tx_stack_pet_psa, tx_stack_ferrite, tx_stack_air]`
  - RX: `g_copper_rx -> [rx_plate_copper]`, `g_ferrite_rx -> [rx_stack_pet_psa, rx_stack_ferrite, rx_stack_air]`
- `stub_port` metadata는 유지한다. `input_stub_body_name` / `output_stub_body_name`은 imported body name이 아니라 pre-unite stub provenance label이다.
- setup-ready mesh target은 conductor-only이며 plate-stack pair에서는 `tx_plate_copper`, `rx_plate_copper`만 사용한다.
- final step/import/mesh payload는 role-level conductor body 하나를 가진다.
  - TX: `tx_plate_copper`
  - RX: `rx_plate_copper`
- group-only continuation은 허용하지 않는다. copper unite 실패, missing united copper body, missing copper group, group membership drift는 즉시 실패한다.

## Acceptance
- generated STEP ledger has exactly 6 final exported body names per plate-stack role.
- generated STEP ledger has both copper and ferrite expected body groups for each plate-stack role.
- final STEP/imported modeled names do not expose `*_copper_wall_t*`, `*_copper_coil_t*`, `*_bridge_s*`, `*_stub_in`, or `*_stub_out`.
- import-only path recreates `g_copper_tx`, `g_copper_rx`, `g_ferrite_tx`, and `g_ferrite_rx` from ledger group metadata.
- setup-ready plate-stack mesh payload contains exactly `tx_plate_copper` and `rx_plate_copper`.
- legacy final copper segment names and generic `SOLID*` drift are export-contract failures, not rename/repair targets.
