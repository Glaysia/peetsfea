# Type2 Port Sheet Contract Rewrite

## Goal
- Active Type2 TxRx single-coil port sheets must be generated from a STEP-time sidecar contract, not from sampled TOML or post-import geometry inference.
- The export ledger is the canonical handoff for resolved port sheet vertices and integration-line endpoints.
- Coordinate drift between the ledger and AEDT-imported bodies must fail before any runtime sheet or port is created.

## Decisions
- Authoring and sampled TOML stay unchanged; port coordinates are not sampled degrees of freedom and no `count=-1` dummy variables are added.
- Single-coil terminal metadata uses `kind = "single_coil_port_v1"` and records `sheet_name`, four global-mm `vertices_xyz`, `integration_line_start_xyz`, and `integration_line_end_xyz`.
- Runtime PyAEDT still creates the sheet primitive, but its coordinates come only from the ledger contract.
- Existing diagonal/sheet-face derivation is removed from the active single-coil path.
- The STEP ledger records hashes for the source TOML and scene STEP so stale or mixed artifacts fail at the import boundary.

## Implementation Boundaries
- Export-side contract ownership lives in [type2_single_coil_ports.py](../code/src/peetsfea/type2_single_coil_ports.py.md), [type2_single_coil_scene.py](../code/src/peetsfea/type2_single_coil_scene.py.md), and [type2_step_ledger.py](../code/src/peetsfea/type2_step_ledger.py.md).
- Import-side validation and runtime sheet creation live in [type2_step_import_ledger.py](../code/src/peetsfea/backend/pyaedt/type2_step_import_ledger.py.md), [type2_step_import_core.py](../code/src/peetsfea/backend/pyaedt/type2_step_import_core.py.md), and [type2_step_import_style.py](../code/src/peetsfea/backend/pyaedt/type2_step_import_style.py.md).
- Port setup must consume the contract through [type2_step_em_input.py](../code/src/peetsfea/backend/pyaedt/type2_step_em_input.py.md) and [type2_step_port_assignment.py](../code/src/peetsfea/backend/pyaedt/type2_step_port_assignment.py.md).
